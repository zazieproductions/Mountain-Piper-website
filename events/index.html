Occasions

Where the pipes make the moment.

Over forty years of performances have taken the pipes to nearly every kind of gathering — Highland games and commencements, church observances and sporting events, regattas and festivals of every stripe. A few favorites:

Festivals & Highland games

Main-stage sets, street performances, and opening flourishes — the instrument that defines the Highland atmosphere, played at a professional standard.

Corporate & civic gatherings

Banquets, conferences, grand openings, and award ceremonies gain instant ceremony with a piper — from a dignified welcome as guests arrive to a show-stopping entrance.

Ceremonies & observances

College commencements, Kirkin’ o’ the Tartans services, Burns Night suppers, St. Andrew’s Day, veterans’ observances, and memorial dedications — traditions the pipes were made to carry.

Private parties & surprises

Milestone birthdays, anniversaries, retirements, and the kind of surprise entrance that becomes family legend. If you can dream it, the pipes can probably do it.

For planners and hosts

Questions organizers ask.

How long can a bagpiper play at an event?

The Great Highland bagpipe is physically demanding, so performances are usually shaped in sets — a stirring 15 to 20 minutes at a time, or several shorter appearances across an event. Tell Mountain Piper the shape of your gathering and you’ll get an honest recommendation for where the pipes will have the most impact.

Can the piper do a surprise entrance?

Yes — it is one of the great joys of the instrument. The sound of the pipes approaching from outside a room, then through the doors, stops every conversation at a banquet, birthday, or awards dinner. Surprise cues are planned discreetly with your organizer or venue.

Can the piper lead a procession or march?

Absolutely. The pipes were built for this: leading graduates into commencement, a wedding party across a lawn, honorees to a stage, or a parade down a festival street. Route, pace, and tune are planned in advance with your team.

What does it cost to hire a bagpiper for an event in Asheville?

Event pricing depends on the length and number of appearances, the location, and the date. Mountain Piper quotes each engagement individually — send the details of your event and you’ll receive a tailored quote.
