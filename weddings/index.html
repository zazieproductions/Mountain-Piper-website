Ceremony moments

Where the pipes fit in your day.

The bagpipe can open a ceremony with quiet gravity or bring it home in full voice. Most couples choose two or three of these moments — Mountain Piper helps you place them where they will mean the most.

Guest arrival & prelude

As guests find their seats, marches and slow airs set an atmosphere no playlist can match — a signal that something out of the ordinary is about to happen.

The processional

The wedding party — and then the entrance everyone is waiting for — led in by the pipes. One of the most requested moments of any wedding day, and for good reason.

The recessional

Newly married, walking back up the aisle to a triumphant tune at full voice. Joyful, unmistakable, and unforgettable on video.

Cocktail hour & reception

Lighter sets as guests mingle, a grand entrance for the couple, or a surprise for the dance floor — the pipes keep the celebration unmistakably yours.

Planned with you

Music shaped around your ceremony — never the reverse.

Every wedding is different: the venue, the season, the size of the room, the readings you have chosen. Mountain Piper starts with your plan and builds the piping into it, so the music feels inevitable rather than inserted.

Western North Carolina gives you remarkable settings for it — garden estates in Asheville, farm venues in the valleys, stone churches, and Blue Ridge overlooks where the pipes carry for miles. Indoors or out, placement and volume are planned so the moment lands exactly right.

Wedding piping, in detail

Questions couples ask.

When during the wedding does the bagpiper play?

Most couples choose some combination of piping as guests arrive, the wedding party processional, the bride’s or couple’s entrance, the recessional, and music during cocktail hour or the reception entrance. Mountain Piper will help you place the pipes where they will land with the most feeling.

Can the piper learn our special song for the ceremony?

Sometimes. The Highland bagpipe plays a fixed nine-note scale, so certain melodies adapt beautifully and others honestly do not. Share the song you have in mind well before the date and you’ll get a straight answer — and, if it won’t translate, alternatives that carry the same emotion.

Are the bagpipes too loud for a small wedding?

Outdoors, almost never — the pipes were built for open ground, and Western North Carolina’s overlooks, gardens, and farm venues suit them perfectly. For small indoor ceremonies, the piper positions at a doorway, balcony, or hallway so the sound arrives with grandeur rather than overwhelming the room.

Will the piper coordinate with our wedding planner or officiant?

Yes. Cues matter — the processional has to start exactly when it should. Your piper will confirm timing and signals with your planner, officiant, or venue coordinator in advance, and again on the day.

How much does a wedding bagpiper cost in Asheville?

Every wedding is quoted individually, because no two are alike: the number of moments piped, the location, and the season all shape the fee. Send the date and venue through the inquiry form and you’ll receive a tailored quote.
