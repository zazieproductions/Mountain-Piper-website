The pipers

Meet Kit Rashid & Alex Stewart.

Two pipers, one shared devotion: traditional Scottish bagpiping and the interceltic music that grew around it.

Kit Rashid, proud to call Asheville home, has spent 43 years as a performer, teacher, and competitor on the Highland bagpipe. He competes as a soloist in the professional grade of the Eastern United States Pipe Band Association and serves as an instructor for the Grandfather Mountain Highlanders, host band of the Grandfather Mountain Highland Games.

Together with Alex Stewart, he brings that standard to every wedding, memorial, festival stage, and lesson — music played the way it was meant to be played, for the people it matters to most.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How far in advance should I book a bagpiper in Asheville?

As early as you can, especially for spring and fall weekends in Western North Carolina. Weddings are often planned many months out, while memorial services can come together in days — either way, reach out and Mountain Piper will let you know promptly whether your date is possible.

Can the piper play a special request or a family tune?

Often, yes. The Highland bagpipe has a fixed nine-note scale, so not every melody translates — but much does, and the repertoire already includes marches, slow airs, hymns, and dance tunes from across the Celtic nations. Share the tune you have in mind and you’ll get an honest answer about whether it can be done well.

What does the piper wear?

Full Highland dress — kilt, sporran, hose, and the rest of the traditional kit — unless you ask for something quieter. For memorial services the dress can be toned down on request.

Can the Great Highland bagpipe play indoors?

Yes, with judgment. It is a powerful outdoor voice, so indoor placement — a balcony, a hallway, a doorway — is chosen to suit the room. For intimate spaces, piping at the threshold as guests arrive or depart often works beautifully. Talk it through when you inquire.

How much does it cost to hire a bagpiper in Asheville?

Every occasion is different — timing, travel, and the shape of the performance all matter — so Mountain Piper quotes each event individually rather than publishing flat rates. Share the date, place, and what you have in mind, and you’ll receive a tailored quote.
